## July 27, 2026 — Initial Rocket Design

**Time Spent:** 2.5 hours

### Work Completed

- Created the initial TARC rocket design in OpenRocket.
- Modeled for two body-tube diameters.
- Added the payload section, transition, booster, motor mount, fins, eggs, parachute, and shock cord.
- Adjusted the design for stability and target altitude.(Motor is not the same one I will be using, this is just a place holder for research)
  once I have my parts, I will be able weigh properly and get true simulated readings.
- Ran an early simulation using an F42T-8.

### Current Results

- Length: 41.406 in
- Mass without motor: 580 g
- Mass with motor: 656 g
- Stability: 3.03 calibers
- Simulated apogee: 822 ft

### Next Steps

- Weigh the real components.
- Update the OpenRocket mass values.
- Reduce total mass below 650 g.
- Test E-class motors. (F class is sending us weigh to high)
- Finalize the recovery and payload layout.
