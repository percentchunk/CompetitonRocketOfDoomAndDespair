## Compatibility Notes

* Confirm that the LOC 2.63-inch outside-diameter tube is accepted as the required T-80 section.
* Measure both LOC tubes before manufacturing the transition and centering rings.
* Confirm that the dual egg protector fits inside the upper LOC tube.
* Enter the measured mass of every component into OpenRocket.
* Keep the final liftoff mass below 650 g.
* Motor, igniter, launch equipment, tools, adhesives, and finishing supplies are excluded.
